### Nudgy

**Nudgy listens to your day and quietly does the next thing.** It's an iPhone assistant (with a Mac app and a web version) that hears what you say and turns it into things that get done, in private beta now.

- **Say it in passing, it's handled:** "remind me to call Mom tonight" sets the reminder; wonder out loud about the weather and the answer is on your Lock Screen a moment later.
- **Answers and briefs:** ask a question, or say "look into…", and a researched brief is waiting when you come back to it.
- **Your apps, with your yes:** it connects to Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, GitHub and Spotify, so it can draft the reply you owe, tell you what's on your plate this week, or turn "I need to fix the login bug" into an issue. Nothing is sent, merged or bought until you say yes.
- **It learns your life:** the people, projects and routines you talk about, and a My Day page of what you talked about, where and with whom.
